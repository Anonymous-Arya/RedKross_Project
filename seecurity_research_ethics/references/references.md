# References and Further Reading

Use these sources as a starting point. Read the relevant material directly, verify current versions, and cite only sources that support the claim being made. Check applicable law and organisational policy.

1. Association for Computing Machinery (ACM). **ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct.**  
   https://www.acm.org/code-of-ethics  
   Relevance: Professional responsibilities, avoiding harm, privacy, and ethical computing.

2. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). **NIST Privacy Framework.**  
   https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework  
   Relevance: Privacy risk management and governance concepts.

3. U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Human Research Protections (OHRP). **Education and Outreach.**  
   https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/education-and-outreach/index.html  
   Relevance: Background on human research protections and informed consent. Applicability depends on study and jurisdiction.

4. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). **Cybersecurity Framework.**  
   https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework  
   Relevance: General cybersecurity risk-management concepts.
