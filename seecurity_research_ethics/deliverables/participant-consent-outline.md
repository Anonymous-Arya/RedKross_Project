# Participant Information and Consent Outline

**Proposed title:** Simulated Cybersecurity User Study: Security Awareness and User Behaviour

## Information to provide
1. **Purpose:** Explore how people interpret fictional cybersecurity examples and security warnings.
2. **Activity:** Review a mock email or warning and answer short questions. State the expected duration before recruitment.
3. **Voluntary choice:** Participation is optional; stopping has no penalty.
4. **Possible discomfort:** A participant may feel uncertain or mildly embarrassed. Feedback must be respectful.
5. **Data collected:** List each data field and why it is needed. Do not request passwords, payment details, or authentication codes.
6. **Data protection:** Explain access, storage, retention, deletion, and intended use.
7. **Withdrawal:** Explain how to stop and any practical limits on removing data after anonymisation or aggregation.
8. **Contact:** Add approved researcher/supervisor contact details.
