# Risk-Mitigation Table

*Ratings are illustrative starting points. Review them with the supervisor for the actual study.*

| Potential risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Participant feels embarrassed | Medium | Medium | Supportive language, private feedback, no public ranking. |
| Unnecessary personal data is collected | Medium | High | Review questions and remove unnecessary fields. |
| Unauthorised access to data | Low | High | Approved secure storage and least-privilege access. |
| Participant feels pressured to join | Medium | Medium | State participation is voluntary and declining has no penalty. |
| Participant becomes uncomfortable | Medium | Medium | Use non-threatening mock content and allow stopping. |
| Simulation is mistaken for a real threat | Low | High | Keep activity controlled and do not send real malicious messages. |
| Data is retained too long | Medium | Medium | Set a retention period and schedule deletion. |
| Purpose is misunderstood | Medium | Medium | Provide clear instructions and a respectful debrief. |
| Data loss or exposure | Low | High | Restrict access and follow incident-reporting procedures. |

The supervisor should confirm ratings, assign mitigation owners, and verify safeguards before the study begins.
