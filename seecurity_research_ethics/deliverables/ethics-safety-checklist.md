# Ethics and Safety Checklist

**Project:** Security Research Ethics — Simulated Cybersecurity User Study  
**Status:** Planning template; review with the supervisor before use.

| Checklist item | Required action | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Informed consent | Explain purpose, activity, data use, risks, contact route, and withdrawal before participation. | To review |
| Voluntary participation | Participation is optional; declining has no penalty. | To review |
| Right to withdraw | Explain how to stop and whether data can be removed after anonymisation or aggregation. | To review |
| Data minimisation | Collect only data needed for the research question. | To review |
| Privacy | Avoid unnecessary identifiers and sensitive information. | To review |
| Safe lab design | Use mock scenarios; no real phishing or unauthorised testing. | To review |
| Access restrictions | Restrict raw data to authorised team members. | To review |
| Retention and deletion | Define retention period and secure deletion method before collection. | To review |
| Debriefing | Explain the simulation and learning points afterward. | To review |
| Participant wellbeing | Avoid shaming, coercion, public ranking, or distressing content. | To review |
| Approval | Obtain required supervisor/ethics approval before recruitment or data collection. | To review |

Do not recruit participants or collect data until the supervisor confirms the design, consent, storage, retention, and any required approval.
