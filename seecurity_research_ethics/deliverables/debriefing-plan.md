# Debriefing Plan

After the simulated activity:

1. Explain the purpose of the study and why a mock scenario was used.
2. Discuss common warning signs such as unexpected requests, urgency, mismatched sender details, unusual links, and requests for sensitive information.
3. Explain how responses will be used and how privacy will be protected.
4. Remind participants not to enter credentials into unfamiliar pages and to use approved reporting channels.
5. Provide a contact route for questions, concerns, or withdrawal requests.
6. Avoid shaming participants or publicly identifying individual performance.

## Sample closing wording
“Thank you for taking part. This was a simulated example intended to explore how people interpret cybersecurity warnings. No real account access or credential collection was part of the activity. The purpose is learning, not judging individual performance. For questions about the study or your responses, contact [approved contact].”
